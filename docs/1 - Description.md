---

## Project Description
---

ProFinder is a mobile and web application that connects customers with qualified professionals for various household services and tasks. The platform allows everyday people to easily find, hire, and manage professional services such as plumbing, electrical work, and other home improvement tasks.

## Core Purpose
---

ProFinder operates as a free marketplace platform where:

- **Customers** can post service requests and receive competitive quotes from qualified professionals
- **Professionals** can discover job opportunities and build their reputation through customer reviews
- **Platform** provides infrastructure for discovery, communication, and reputation management without processing payments

## Key Features
---

- User authentication (email, Google OAuth)
- Professional profile creation with portfolio and service categories
- Order creation and management system
- Real-time messaging between customers and professionals
- Advanced search and filtering
- Reviews and ratings system
- Moderator tools for content management and dispute resolution
- Notification system (push, email, in-app)

## Target Market
---

Service industry professionals (plumbers, electricians, painters, cleaners) and homeowners seeking reliable service providers.
